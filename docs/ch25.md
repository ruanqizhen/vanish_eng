# Chapter 25: God-Tier Merge — Hard-Rewriting the Script

Cole's fingers became afterimages over the typewriter. He took every fragment of Dorian in his mind — the Inquisitor glancing back with a smile in the snow; the CEO, wineglass in hand, gentle before the window; the NPC in the rented apartment, clumsily boiling noodles — and forcibly **code-merged** them like a programmer merging branches.

Three keycaps launched from the keyboard. From the vacant sockets, golden virtual keys auto-generated.

He wrote Dorian's new definition on the golden paper:

*Character Reconstruction — Target: Dorian Cross (Three-Lifetime Memory Fusion). Soul Substrate: Loyalty (Inquisitor Worldline) · Devotion (CEO Worldline) · Sincerity (NPC Worldline). New Designation: First Guardian of the Narrative Engine. Core Anchor: Eternal Bond — Soul Contract with Author Cole Vance, indivisible, unerasable. Authority Tier: HIGHEST — Equal to Author.*

With every word he wrote, blood seeped from another orifice. The system Engine was backlashing against the causal threads word by word. But Cole's hands blurred into afterimages.

*"Why is it always you saving me? This time — I'm the one who saves you!"*

Error dialogs overwhelmed the screen: `0x7A3F — Setting Conflict. 0x7A40 — Soul Data Incompatible. 0x7A41 — Single entity holds both "Tool" and "Guardian" definitions. 0x7A42 — Guardian rank requires Narrative Engine core recognition.`

Cole hand-wrote annotations after every error: *OVERRULED. OVERRULED. OVERRULED.*

The sky-spanning blue points of light — Dorian's scattered fragments — were summoned by the highest authority. They surged from every ruin, every corner, even from the light in a little girl's eyes. They re-wove into bone, meridian, viscera, muscle, skin.

In a blaze of golden light, Dorian's eyes snapped open.

He looked down at Cole — kneeling, bleeding from every orifice — and said, softly, "You've gone and wrecked yourself again."

Before they could embrace, Vera let out a desperate roar. She swallowed the Narrative Engine's remaining core — a crystalline orb radiating prismatic light — in a single gulp. The fractures in her plain gray dress leaked blinding brilliance.

"The story isn't over yet. The ending — isn't yours to decide."
