# Chapter 23: The Path-Cleaver Who Overclocked His Base Kernel

The chains dragged Dorian toward the void within the tower. Through the Sympathy link, Cole vomited mouthfuls of blood mixed with code-fragments. In the cracks of his skin, not red muscle tissue showed through, but flickering blue data-streams. The formatting force was eroding his existence.

Dorian looked at him. And smiled.

He closed his eyes. Began the Sympathy Law. He was the enforcer. The antivirus software. There was a base-level backdoor in his body — when he activated it, the Sympathy flow reversed. He no longer passed wounds to Cole. He absorbed *every injury and every pain from Cole's body into himself.*

"Every enforcer has a logic backdoor. It lets the system downgrade us into a 'data pipe.'" Dorian's voice grew lighter, faster. His body was being consumed from within by blue radiance. "A pipe has no processing logic of its own. It just lets data flow through. All the formatting commands. All the deletion markers. All the backlash — I'll take it. You just need — to keep going."

"Cole." He smiled. Tears spilled. The tears were blue. "I've spent my whole damn life being a tool. This time — *I choose.*"

His entire body ignited with ghost-blue soul-true-fire. Not combustion — the base-level record of his own existence being deleted, line by source code. He palmed Cole through the golden gates of the Endpoint Tower.

Cole tumbled through. Looked back.

Dorian's body — amid endless black chains — glowed from within like porcelain. Every fissure glowing blue from inside. He detonated into a shower of brilliant blue points of light. The lights rose into the sky like a meteor shower.

A small portion drifted into the city ruins. They settled on the shoulders of other awakened ones. A little girl looked up. Watched blue light streak across the sky. In her eyes, for the first time — something that wasn't fear ignited.

Cole looked down. Dorian's handless pocket watch rested at his feet. He picked it up. Gripped it desperately in his palm.

Then he turned. And faced the core of the Endpoint Tower.
