## About the Novel

*Vanishing Horizon* is a cross-dimensional epic that fuses urban, Lovecraftian horror, gamified stat mechanics, alternate history, arcane cultivation, and hard-science higher-dimensional warfare.

Cole Vance, a bottom-tier delivery courier, receives an order on a storm-drenched night that points to a blank zone on the map. What appears to be an ordinary delivery plunges him into a world controlled by the "Narrative Engine" — a false universe that can be hot-reloaded, dimensionally formatted, and script-rewritten at will.

His encounter with the cold enforcer Dorian Cross binds two lonely souls across countless death loops, forging a bond that transcends life and death. From cracking rule-based horror with a programmer's mindset, to consuming an Arcane Core and cultivating; from navigating the court politics of the Valdrian Empire, to facing down higher-dimensional enforcers in a cosmic war — Cole Vance uses the hard logic of a coder who exploits bugs to tear open the world's truths, layer by layer.

When the Endpoint typewriter rises before him at the end, he finally understands: true free will is not becoming the god of a world — it is refusing the allure of godhood, and choosing to be a mortal of flesh and blood.

### The Three Falls and Rises of a Life

- **Shattered Opulence: Twilight of a Billion-Dollar Legacy** — Born into a wealthy family of investment magnates in Manhattan's Upper East Side. At age eight, his father perished in a mysterious flash-crash of Wall Street algorithmic trading. A billion-dollar estate was liquidated by creditors within weeks. His mother took him to a damp, cramped basement flat in South London. Under the dim glow of single-bulb lamps, clutching his father's sole legacy — a stopped mechanical pocket watch — he completed his first foray into the logic of reality.
- **Golden Boy, Fallen Architect** — Through sheer obsession, he fought his way into the Computer Science program at MIT. After graduation, he joined a Silicon Valley tech giant and, within five years, rose to Principal Infrastructure Architect, designing systems handling petabytes of data for billions of active users. He married a prominent tech researcher and lived at the peak of the industry elite. But at the height of his career, he refused to sign off on a black-hat data-harvesting algorithm designed to exploit the platform's entire user base. In retaliation, upper management fabricated corporate espionage charges against him. Overnight, he was blacklisted across Silicon Valley, stripped of stock options, and buried under a ruinous, multi-million-dollar non-compete lawsuit.
- **The Golden Courier** — With every corporate door slammed shut, this architect who once orchestrated global data backbones was forced to shed his tailored suits and pull on a yellow high-vis delivery vest. Straddling a beat-up electric scooter, he became an ordinary courier navigating the storm-drenched streets of Grayharbor. Sixteen-hour shifts of mechanical deliveries showed him — from the absolute bottom — the brutal exploitation of labor by the algorithms of reality.

### Why He Wrote

Due to a draconian non-compete agreement, K.H. Swan was legally barred from touching anything related to software development. Stripped of the right to code, he made an insane decision — if he couldn't write code in a terminal, he would build the universe inside a novel!

In noisy restaurant waiting areas and on the back of his electric scooter in the rain, he pulled out a phone with a shattered screen. Using the logic forged in his computer science years, he treated words as variables, horror protocols as bugs, and tapped out *Vanishing Horizon* character by character. He wasn't just writing a novel — he was building a virtual system, millions of words strong, designed to debug the Engine of fate itself.

His debut was hailed by mainstream literary critics as "the ultimate roar of human free will from within the digital prison — 2025's most defiant act of creation."
