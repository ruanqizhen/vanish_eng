# Chapter 15: Physical Exorcism on the Solar Court

The void shattered. Cole felt his body torn apart, reassembled, torn apart again by countless forces.

The pain transcended flesh — as if someone was patching his source code directly. Every line of memory. Every identity tag. Forcibly overwritten. His vision flooded with garbled light-shadows: the territorial map of the Valdrian Empire. The destiny-star charts of every civil and military official. The thousand-year reign of the dragon throne in the Solar Court. This information flooded his consciousness like a deluge.

Then he crashed onto cold, hard golden-brick flooring.

The great hall fell instantly silent.

Cole pushed himself up and saw a palace so vast it stole breath. Hundred-yard golden pillars coiled with living five-clawed dragon reliefs. Pale golden halos of spiritual energy pulsed in their eyes. The ceiling had no discernible limit — only churning cloud-seas and a grand stellar array within. On both sides stood civil and military officials whose auras were abyss-deep. Their combined spiritual pressure wove an invisible net.

He recognized this place. The Solar Court of the Valdrian Empire.

And he was kneeling at its dead center.

"INSOLENCE!" A roar detonated through the hall. Cole's fingers instinctively touched his chest — the cold weight of the pocket watch pressed through the fabric of his official robes. Dorian wasn't here. Dorian was on the other side of the rift. Cole was alone. He wore the robes of the Valdrian First Rank High Justiciar. He faced the assembled might of the Empire. Alone.

He moved his hand from the watch to the hilt at his waist. The Crown Blade. His eyes changed.

At the head of the civil officials' column stood a middle-aged man in violet-gold python robes. His face was jade-smooth. Three long strands of beard flowed down his chest. But his eyes — those eyes held two abyssal pools with no visible bottom.

Around him swirled visible violet official-aura — the rank-suppression by a Valdrian First Rank minister. It was so dense it had nearly congealed. Above his head, it formed a canopy of violet-gold radiance. And more terrifying: his system privilege had reached the Apotheosis Stage — Administrator Rank. One step from total system sovereignty.

Only one man in the Empire wore violet-gold python robes: Grand Regent Malrik Vane.

The name activated a flood of associated memory — Malrik Vane, elder statesman across three dynasties. His disciples and protégés blanketed the court. They called it "the Vane Half-Court." Outwardly loyal to the throne. In truth, he'd long since consolidated the Empire's entire power network into his private possession. Any official who opposed him was either exiled to the frontiers or simply vanished into some deep night.

But Cole knew more. This Grand Regent's true identity went far beyond corrupt minister — he was a *Narrative Engine patch program avatar.* A tool the world used to correct plot trajectories. Every previous transdimensional traveler had failed because they'd triggered Malrik Vane — this "plot-lock" — and been crushed by the world's entire rule-force.

"High Justiciar Cole Vance! Failing to kneel before the sovereign! Breach of court protocol! What punishment does this deserve?!"

Malrik Vane's voice wasn't loud. Yet it carried a Law-like authority. Every word seemed etched into the hall's very space, making the air itself vibrate.

Cole's knees softened fractionally. An invisible force — mountainous — pressed down on his shoulders. Trying to drive him to the floor. He bit through the tip of his tongue. The spike of pain jolted him. He forced his spine straight.

He looked down at his robes. Black fabric embroidered with the hidden patterns of the Judgment Beast. A crimson jade belt at his waist. A black-gauze official's cap on his head, a single white plume inserted in its band. The formal attire of the Valdrian First Rank High Justiciar.

High Justiciar — the Empire's supreme adjudicator. Keeper of laws and punishments. Corrector of the hundred ministries. Rank-equivalent to Deputy Chancellor. Wielder of the mandate to "patrol the realm on Heaven's behalf."

And on that jade belt hung a sword less than three feet long. Its scabbard was lacquer-black. Unadorned. Yet it radiated a pressure that made the entire hall's air congeal.

The Crown Blade.

Above the corrupt sovereign. Below the corrupt minister. One blade to execute them all.

Cole's mouth curved faintly.

"I ask the Grand Regent —" Cole's voice was quiet. But it carried to every corner of the hall. "— you charge me with protocol violation. I charge you with embezzlement. Treason. Manipulation of the throne. Ten counts. Each warranting execution."

The hall erupted.

"You dare?!" A Vane protégé — the Vice Minister of Personnel — lunged forward. But Cole was already unrolling a bamboo scroll. Dense columns of figures covered its surface.

"Your legitimate annual salary totals one million, four hundred thirty thousand silver taels over thirteen years. Your actual assets — fields, shops, estates — are valued at over thirty million. A twenty-fold gap. Would the Grand Regent care to explain the mathematics?"

Malrik Vane's face went rigid.

"I'll explain it for you." Cole's voice accelerated. "Your family started with five hundred taels. In forty-five years, you multiplied that sixty thousand times. That's a compound annual growth rate that's mathematically impossible under any legitimate commercial model. Your wealth isn't just unexplained — it's *impossible.*"

He raised his finger. Pale blue mathematical symbols materialized in the air before him. "Let's model your governance function. f(t, a, c) — where t is time, a is authority allocation, and c is system integrity. Your resource chain rests on a fraudulent foundation. Which means —" The symbols rotated. Accelerated. "— your function's second derivative is negative. Your memory allocation is leaking. The Sovereign Leviathan can *feel* the discrepancy."

Above them, the towering manifestation of the Empire's collective will — the Sovereign Leviathan — let out a confused roar. Its connection to Malrik Vane was faltering. The math was indisputable. Heaven's Law could not refuse calculus.

"NO!" Malrik Vane's composure shattered. He thrust a palm-strike at Cole — Apotheosis-level force, enough to reduce a city block to rubble.

The Sovereign Leviathan intercepted it.

The Sovereign Leviathan had switched sides.

Cole drew the Crown Blade. Silver-white data-lightning flickered along the edge. "Malrik Vane. By the authority vested in me as High Justiciar — I adjudicate you guilty. The sentence is termination."

He moved. The blade came down.

It didn't cut flesh. It cut *code.* Malrik Vane's body dissolved not into blood — but into writhing black ribbons of patch-program fragments, sprawling meters across the golden-brick floor.

The court was silent.

Cole sheathed the Crown Blade. The system notification appeared in his vision:

**[Plot Thread 5 Unlocked: Patch Collection Quest — Locate remaining Patch Program Code Fragments (0/7).]**

In the highest shadows of the Solar Court, a woman in a plain gray dress stood motionless. A celadon thermos cradled in her fingertips. Vera Ashworth watched the execution in silence. Then her gaze met — across the vast hall — another pair of eyes. Gray. Cold. Dorian stood in the shadow of a dragon pillar.

Their gazes collided for one soundless second.

Then Vera turned. Vanished. Only a single line drifted down — spoken so softly only Dorian could hear:

*"You're still as stubborn as you ever were."*
