# Chapter 22: The Courier Ignores Terrain

A hundred-meter wall of flesh spanned the approach to the Endpoint Tower. Countless eyes of different colored light opened across its surface — scarlet, ghost-green, corpse-white, dark-gold. Their intersecting gazes formed lethality-grade sanity contamination.

Dorian pulled out the tattered *Anomalous Food Service Regulations Handbook* and recited Article 47. The white radiance briefly purged the contamination — but Vera drew a circle in the air and overwrote his rule.

Cole pulled out his phone. The casing was scorched-black and cracked. The screen still glowed — faintly, stubbornly. At its center: a delivery interface. *Endpoint Tower Clearance Order.*

He raised it one-handed. Inside him, the Alchemical Kernel spun at speed. Phone and Kernel resonated at the same frequency. The order's contractual force and the law of causality resonated.

A vast voice resounded in every heart across the city. Sourceless. Directionless. As if Grayharbor itself was speaking:

*"Urban Courier Protocol — Conceptual Tier — Activation Confirmed. Courier ID: GB-0721. Current Status: En Route. Active Order: Endpoint Tower Clearance Order. Law Adjudication: Delivery Priority — HIGHEST TIER. Law Override: No physical, legal, or rule-based obstacle within city limits may impede a courier currently en route. Special Note — This clause overrides ALL priorities. Including the rules of gods."*

Golden light expanded from the point where scooter tires touched the ground. It spread along pavement. Climbed walls. Covered tentacles. Wherever the gold light reached — the eyes closed one by one. Tentacles withered. Magical effects auto-dispelled. Not destroyed — *overruled.*

The scooter rode up the vertical hundred-meter wall. It didn't — it *passed through* from the conceptual layer. Vera's was physically intact. But before the concept "the delivery route cannot be obstructed" — it was indistinguishable from nonexistent.

Cole and Dorian, a single golden meteor, pierced straight through.

The instant the breakout succeeded — countless pitch-black data chains fired from within the tower. They hooked into Dorian's shoulders. Ripped him from the back seat.

The delivery phone sounded its final alert. The screen went dark. Overloaded. Burned out.
