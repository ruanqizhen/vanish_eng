# Chapter 20: Counterattack on Blank Paper

Vera Ashworth deployed the ultimate dimensional strike.

A twelve-kilometer radius of downtown Grayharbor was forcibly Z-axis. Everything — flattened into a boundless sheet of white paper. Cole became a pencil-sketch-like 2D silhouette. Dorian: an outline edged in faint dark-gold. No up. No down. No depth. No mass. In two-dimensional space, the concept of *attack* itself didn't exist.

Vera's true form manifested: a skyscraper-tall code-humanoid built of flowing green strings, every line refreshing in real time. *"Lower-realm insects. In my world, you are two ink-dots on a sheet of paper. I can erase you with an eraser."*

Rust's voice crackled through a collapse drive: *"The chip is already burned — but Cole! You're two-dimensional! Your body is compressed into information-state! You can rewrite yourself using information!"* She fired higher-dimensional data at his coordinates using Old Max's chip's final write-access. Blue smoke rose from the base. *"—Goodbye, teacher."*

Simultaneously, in the physical server room, Ada Li blocked Vera's remote attack-tentacles with her life. If that server went down, the coordinates would lose their anchor.

On the 2D plane, Cole dragged his right hand — now just a gray outline being slowly erased — and pulled the Narrative Pen from his pocket. The dark gold barrel erupted with radiance whiter than the paper itself.

Vera screamed. For the first time — real pain. She mobilized every code-tentacle to intercept his write-access. Dorian raised a golden barrier. Blocked the side and frontal attacks. But the vertically-descending tentacles pierced through.

"Eighteen seconds," Dorian said. "That's all I can hold."

Tentacles coiled around Cole's right arm. The gray line began turning white — the color of information being erased.

Cole thought with a programmer's mind. He slashed a horizontal line across the white paper. Beside it, he wrote a single code comment:

`// A third dimension exists here.`

*BOOM —*

The white paper tore open. Golden lines ripped through the 2D plane. Three-dimensional space erupted like bamboo after rain — golden walls bursting from beneath the paper, shredding, crushing, incinerating Vera's code-tentacles in swaths. The white-paper firmament was punched through from within. Beyond the hole: the ruins of Grayharbor, already two-thirds formatted.

Vera retreated to her main version. Her code-humanoid form destabilizing wildly. She issued one final command:

*"Full formatting has been initiated. When the sun rises tomorrow — no living thing remains on the surface!"*
