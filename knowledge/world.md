# Endpoint System Laws & World-Building Dictionary

*Vanishing Horizon* operates on a reality controlled by a higher-dimensional system called the **Narrative Engine**. In this world, reality is a simulated universe rendered in real time by code, laws, and narrative modules. This is the authoritative dictionary of the Endpoint System, core laws, and world-building mechanics.

---

## I. Core System Architecture

### 1. The Narrative Engine
- **Underlying Physics**: Fundamentally a precision probability-control system. It rewrites quantum-state distributions at the microscopic scale, thereby exerting directed influence over macroscopic physical reality — manifesting as "the story currently being told."
- **Modular Hot-Reload (DLL Extension Mode)**: The Engine contains at least eleven core Law modules (referred to as `.dll` dynamic link library files). Each module represents a complete set of physical/supernatural rules (e.g., the Courier Protocol, the Aetheric Code Protocol, the RPG Stat Law, etc.). The system can dynamically load, unload, hot-swap, and force-sync these modules at will.
- **The Gestalt Protocol**: A protocol used by the Narrative Engine to lock core plot anchors and NPC memories. When anomalous variables (such as trans-dimensional travelers) trigger worldview disruptions, the Gestalt Protocol forcibly suppresses or rewrites relevant characters' memories to maintain logical coherence across the world.

### 2. The Endpoint System & Endpoint Tower
- **Core Definition**: The Endpoint System is the Engine's self-healing and garbage-collection (GC) logic hub, specifically designed to detect logical loopholes, purge redundant threads, and execute a "Grand Reset" of spacetime in the event of catastrophic collapse.
- **The Endpoint Tower**: The system's logical projection into three-dimensional space. Visually, it manifests as a column of pure black data-streams, uneven in thickness, its surface covered in flickering garbled characters and core symbols (such as "ERASE" and "RESET"). It executes hard-formatting of the city's terrain and physical laws by emitting high-energy pulses.

## II. The Three Conceptual-Level Core Laws

### 1. The Urban Courier Protocol (Delivery Law)
The most fundamental and highest-priority operational module. Originally the underlying logic governing bottom-tier gig-economy couriers, it was later weaponized by Cole Vance using "programmer's intuition," evolving into a conceptual-level defense and breach technique that ignores physics.

- **【First Law: Optimal Route Doctrine】** — The system-planned delivery route is, under all current constraints, always the most efficient and shortest path available.
- **【Second Law: Unbreakable Chain Doctrine】** — The transport chain from pickup to delivery enjoys the system's highest-priority rule protection. Any object obstructing delivery (whether human walls, security personnel, or incoming bullets) is classified by the system as a "route obstacle," automatically triggering environmental accommodation (bullets grazing past, enemies inexplicably slipping) — achieving conceptual-level "collision avoidance" and physical-line breach.
- **【Third Law: Inevitable Delivery Doctrine (Forced Dispatch)** — A named mandatory dispatch cannot be canceled or transferred. Once accepted, the delivery must be completed and physically signed for within the time limit. Timeout or refusal to sign triggers the system's garbage-collection routine (direct erasure of the target).

### 2. System-Enforced Empathy Synchronization Lock
Originally a tongue-in-cheek safety protocol or relationship fail-safe built on biometric emotional keys written by Cole's parents as a satirical corporate-dystopian override. Due to spatial overlap in the Iron Citadel's spacetime, the Engine forced this symbiotic lock.

- **【Contact Trigger Clause】** — Activated immediately whenever the two parties maintain physical contact (even through clothing) for more than **3 seconds**.
- **【Intimacy Validation Handshake】** — Once triggered, the bound party must, within **30 seconds**, perform "Intimacy Validation Handshakes" or "Dopamine Compliance Queries" (romantic dialogue queries) satisfactory to the system. Failure to satisfy the compiler triggers a lethal-intensity **Empathy Overload Strike**.
- **【Pain Sync & Life-Sympathy】** — The two parties share HP bars, injury status, and 100% pain perception. When one sustains fatal damage, the other shares the wound via the red-thread brand, ensuring neither soul is fully extinguished.
- **【Data-Channel One-Way Transfer (Reverse Sympathy)**】 — A special backdoor exploit unique to Dorian Cross as an antivirus program. When one party actively degrades their own attribute to "data pipe" and designates the other as "primary process," all formatting and deletion damage is unidirectionally transferred to the pipe, protecting the primary process's survival.

### 3. Gamified Stat Law (The RPG Protocol)
A secondary rule module that forcibly imposes a quantified stat-sheet sandbox onto the physical world.

- **【Attribute Quantification Clause】** — All physical metrics (strength, speed, stamina, etc.) are rendered as visible numerical values (HP, MP, Level). 18 points of Strength can push a heavy truck; reaching 0 Stamina induces severe physical slowdown penalties.
- **【Collision Deadlock Glitch (CPU Starvation)】** — The sandbox system has a minimum physical-unit threshold when detecting multi-entity spatial overlap ("1 pixel"). Cole Vance exploited this by forcing monsters into a detection gap exactly 1 pixel wide between two trucks, causing the physics engine to suffer an infinite hitbox-resolution loop (CPU starvation), forcing the system to purge the overloaded entities and punch a hole through the logic layer.
